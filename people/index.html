Lab life

Conferences, awards, graduations and visits: what the year looks like in the lab.

  1. July 2026

    Best paper, 2nd place, at ILAIS 2026

    Eylon Yehiel's multimodal pricing paper with Anat Goldstein, at the Peres Academic Center.

  2. June 2026

    Rector's and Dean's lists

    Yarin Amar on the Rector's List and Eylon Yehiel on the Dean's List for academic excellence.

  3. 2026

    Graduations

    Yehonatan Zion (PhD, generative AI) and Yona Elbar (thesis MSc, multimodal pipelines) graduated.

  4. February 2026

    Budapest, both ways

    A new grant for our federated learning collaboration with CrySyS Lab, after Chen's two visits to Budapest. Next, we host them in Ariel.

  5. January 2026

    IEEE CCNC in Las Vegas

    Two talks: QoE prediction for Call of Duty, and dynamic GNNs for cloud anomaly detection.

  6. 2026–27

    GPU lab sessions

    In Chen's machine learning course, students run the same pipelines on NVIDIA GPUs with RAPIDS.

Join the lab

We take PhD and MSc students who want to build machine learning that holds up after deployment. These topics are open now. Supervision and funding depend on the project, so write first and we will talk it through.

  • Adaptive encrypted-traffic AI Retrieval, out-of-distribution detection, QUIC and post-quantum traffic. Email about this topic: Adaptive encrypted-traffic AI
  • Adversarial and trustworthy ML Robust models for security, healthcare and multimodal systems. Email about this topic: Adversarial and trustworthy ML
  • Clinical machine learning Decision support, personalization, privacy and responsible validation. Email about this topic: Clinical machine learning
  • Multimodal retrieval Vision-language representations for products, places and recommendations. Email about this topic: Multimodal retrieval
  • AI and mechanism design Incentives, marketplaces, teams and resource allocation. Email about this topic: AI and mechanism design
